About Us

FCSR is a forward-thinking educational institution focused on empowering students through creativity and IT skills. Based in the Free City of Igralandia, we provide inclusive and modern education designed for the digital age.

Our Programs

Our school offers a diverse curriculum with a strong emphasis on computer science, digital arts, problem-solving, critical thinking, and innovation. Students engage in hands-on learning, coding, design, and project-based collaboration.
